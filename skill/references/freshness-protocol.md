# Freshness Protocol

1. Prefer an official employer career page or official ATS record.
2. Record the visible posting date or machine-readable date field.
3. Classify the date as VERIFIED only when it comes from the employer, ATS, or another durable source record.
4. Treat snippets, aggregator badges, and search-index dates as INFERRED.
5. Do not call an opportunity fresh when the date is missing or cannot be verified.
6. Default to roles posted within the candidate's configured freshness window, commonly seven days.
